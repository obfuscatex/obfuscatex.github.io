Data Deletion

✦
Last Updated: August 29, 2026
📋  Quick Summary

Bible – Chapter by Chapter stores your reading progress only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts. You can delete all your data instantly from your device, or request deletion via email. Deletion is completed within 7 days.

1

Delete Directly on Your Device (Instant)

The fastest way — no email needed:

  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Apps → Bible – Chapter By Chapter
  3. Tap Storage → Clear Data (or Clear Storage)
  4. Alternatively, simply uninstall the app — this removes all local data

This immediately deletes your reading progress, quiz scores, notification preferences, and reading streaks from your device.

2

Request Deletion via Email

If you need assistance or want confirmation that no server-side data exists for your device, contact us at:

obfuscatexapps@gmail.com

✉  Send Deletion Request Email

We will confirm by reply email that no account or server data is associated with your installation.

3

What Data Is Deleted

Reading progress (chapters completed)
Quiz scores and history
Notification preferences and reminder settings
Reading streaks

All data is stored locally via Android DataStore. We do not operate servers that store your reading data, and we do not create accounts.

Advertising: Ads are served by Appodeal and its mediated networks. Ad-related data (advertising ID, IP) is governed by Appodeal's Privacy Policy and respective network policies. To reset your advertising ID, go to device Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID.

4

What Happens After Your Request

We confirm that no server-side account exists (the app is offline-first)
We provide instructions to clear local data if not already done
We reply by email to confirm completion
Timeline: Response and confirmation within 7 days (usually within 48 hours)
